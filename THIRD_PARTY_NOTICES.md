# Third-party assets

The Gemini icon is loaded from the user-selected Google favicon endpoint:

https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?sz=128&domain=gemini.google.com

Icon requests do not include search or conversation data, and the image elements
use `referrerpolicy="no-referrer"`.
